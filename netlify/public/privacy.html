Quite Frankly app: privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

The Quite Frankly app is run by DahmsIO LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 13376001, registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, London, WC2H 9JQ, England). We are the controller of your personal data. Contact: hello@dahms.io. ICO registration: pending.

What the app stores, and why

  • Your email address: the app no longer offers sign-in and does not ask for your email. If you signed in on an earlier test version, we still hold your email address and account data until you ask us to delete them.
  • A notification token and your notification choices (live, new video, Culture Club), only if you allow notifications: so we can send you the alerts you chose. It is a device token and is not linked to your email address.
  • Bug reports and feature requests you send from the app: your text, an optional screenshot you choose, and basic device information. They are not linked to your email address. Bug reports are kept for up to 6 months and are then deleted.
  • AskFrankie AI (an optional feature, when switched on): opens inside the app as a guest, and the app sends AskFrankie nothing about you. What AskFrankie stores is described in its own privacy notice. Before your first question you must agree that your questions are processed by third-party AI providers (Anthropic and Voyage AI).

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking in the app, and we don't sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Netlify: hosts our servers and the small database that holds the items above.
  • Expo (push notification service), with Apple and Google: delivers notifications to your device.
  • YouTube, Twitch, Rumble, SoundCloud, Vimeo and Spotify (players and links in the app), the Quite Frankly website (run on Squarespace) and other sites the app links to (for example Patreon, SubscribeStar, PayPal, Amazon and social networks): when you play or open their content they handle your data under their own policies.
  • AskFrankie and its AI providers (Anthropic, Voyage AI), as above.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app’s YouTube features you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Some of these providers are based in the US, and the AskFrankie database is hosted in the US. Where we send personal data there, we rely on the provider's UK-US data bridge certification or on the standard data-protection clauses (the UK addendum) in its data processing terms. We send each provider only what it needs.

How long we keep it

If you signed in on an earlier test version, your account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Deleting it removes your email address, all your sign-in sessions, any pending sign-in code, this device's notification registration and your linked AskFrankie account. Bug reports are not linked to you and are not removed by account deletion. Our AskFrankie database is backed up weekly to a private Google Drive folder; backups are kept for up to about six weeks and then overwritten, so deleted AskFrankie data can remain in a backup for that long. We make no separate backups of the app's sign-in data; our hosting provider may keep its own short-term backups.

Delete your data

The app no longer has accounts. To have an earlier account or any other data deleted, use the web deletion page (we email you a code to prove the address is yours) or email hello@dahms.io.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, take a copy, or withdraw consent, at any time, by emailing hello@dahms.io. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The Quite Frankly app is for people aged 13 or over. AskFrankie AI is for people aged 18 or over.

Changes

If we change how we use your data we will update this page and, if the change matters, tell you in the app.
